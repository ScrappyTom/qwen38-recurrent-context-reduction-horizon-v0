# Construction-set counterfactual audit

Date: 2026-08-20

Status: **offline audit complete; accumulated receipt chronology blocks raw governing-set co-residency, while a fresh sectional construction phase is physically feasible**

## Question

Did the late recurrent-reduction turns contain a stable construction-ready set, and if not, could the four governing documents and exact candidate sections coexist under the frozen 25,088-token context with the same 4,096-token response reserve?

This audit made no model call. It reconstructed exact packets, restored exact receipt backing, rendered the exact parent chat template, and counted with the qualified Qwen3.8 tokenizer projection. The two actual call-12 prompts reproduced the provider counts exactly before any counterfactual was accepted.

Fit is only a physical result. The audit does not claim that any declared target set is semantically sufficient or predict what Qwen would do if shown it.

## Counterfactual surfaces

Three surfaces were frozen at design commit `39ff888dab9459486a3f75d2536cb71ea45be5dd`:

1. **Historical restore only:** use the actual call-12 chronology and restore one exact full occurrence of every required object without removing anything else.
2. **Receipt floor, then restore:** demote every remaining full result body with positive exact-token savings, then restore the required objects.
3. **Fresh phase:** retain the exact system/tool schema, task, complete structural outline, and one exact action/result pair for every required object; omit prior chronology. This is a capacity lower bound, not an observed state.

The governing set was the exact `RESULTS.md` and `DIRECT_TRANSCRIPT_AUDIT.md` from each of the two studies. Target groups ranged from one region through the exact four-region top-level tail R030–R033.

Therefore, reuse-aware selection among full result bodies cannot by itself assemble all four documents inside either late chronology. The physical blocker is now the accumulated action/receipt history as well as raw result residency.

The 69-token miss does not establish a need for semantic compression. A very small mechanical serialization reduction could make that particular packet fit. Conversely, this audit did not test the complete 970-line target, which is not available through one legal exact read and would be a materially larger construction surface.

## What Qwen actually had

The late actors had exact recoverability but not stable semantic co-residency. Receipt objects exposed identity, hashes, source binding, and an exact reopen action; they contained no result content or digest. Seed 42 rotated among governing documents while retaining only a subset of their bodies. Seed 314159 first held the governing documents while target coverage was partial, then acquired target text while governing content left residency, and ended with source bodies returning after target bodies had become receipts.

Because every assistant output was one bare action and no note/checkpoint surface existed, no model-authored synthesis persisted when a raw result was replaced. The observed loop is therefore compatible with a rational response to a repeatedly incomplete visible set; it is not clean evidence that Qwen saw a complete construction state and refused to act.

## Interpretation

Strongest supported conclusions:

- The current append-only action/receipt chronology cannot hold all four governing documents at the late boundary, even after every remaining positive-savings full result is demoted.
- A fresh exact construction surface can hold all four governing documents plus one target region, or the three-region R031–R033 tail, with the original reserve.
- The four-region R030–R033 tail is nearly feasible but exceeds the exact envelope by 69 tokens under the frozen serialization.
- Reuse-aware eviction may reduce churn, but it cannot solve the demonstrated accumulated-chronology limit by changing only which full result body is resident.

Not established:

- that R031–R033 is semantically sufficient;
- that Qwen would mutate when presented with the fresh set;
- that digestion, summaries, model-controlled pinning, or a phase reset improves behavior;
- that the model's continued acquisition was unnecessary; or
- that the whole target can be constructed from one prompt.

The audit shifts the next causal question. A pure reopen-protection experiment would address churn but leave the proven chronology overhead intact. The more diagnostic next comparison is a separately authorized construction-phase representation: exact governing evidence plus a bounded exact target surface, with old chronology externally custodied rather than carried as individual receipts. A semantic digest or model-authored checkpoint should remain a separate treatment because it changes information content, not only residency.

No successor inference was selected, authorized, or run by this audit.
