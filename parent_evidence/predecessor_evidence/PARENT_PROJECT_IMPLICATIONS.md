## New standalone observation

- preserved the exact historical acquisition in both seeds;
- made the exact requested result deliverable with the original 4,096-token reserve;
- left +154 and +395 tokens of reserved headroom;
- supported one further decision, which was another target acquisition in both seeds;
- produced no mutation, check, submission, or candidate change.

A much more aggressive recent-two retention rule saved 6,956–9,236 tokens but changed both immediate decisions. Both actors sought a source result that the policy had demoted, including one exact reopen request.
