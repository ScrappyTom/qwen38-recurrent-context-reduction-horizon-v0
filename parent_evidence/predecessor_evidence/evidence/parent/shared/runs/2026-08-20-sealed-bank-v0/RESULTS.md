# Qwen3.8 large-world local operability v0: results

Date: 2026-08-20

Status: **valid intermediate result; structural addressability changed
acquisition, but no condition achieved local operability**

## Result in one sentence

On one authentic 57,976-byte maintained artifact and two matched Qwen3.8
seeds, the compact mechanical outline changed acquisition from complete
target reconstruction plus one governing source to selective target reads
plus all four governing sources; all four actors nevertheless reached the
25,088-token execution boundary before mutation.

## Frozen comparison

The bank used the exact approved handoff with SHA-256
`cb584097733ee8c18fbe82b94bfd5bb4ebc0765c294632b95a096965a32bfde2`:

- Qwen3.8-27B AD-IQ2_S;
- full 66/66 GPU offload;
- q8 K/V cache;
- 25,088-token actual context;
- MTP and preserved reasoning off;
- a complete 4,096-token response reserve before every inference;
- seeds 42 and 314159;
- C0 ordinary exact access versus S1 the same access plus a complete
  mechanically derived 33-region target outline and `read_region`;
- 24 calls per cell, 96 aggregate maximum, one attempt, and no retries; and
- no model-facing semantic checker or host-selected target region.

The four cells produced 42 model responses, below the 96-call authorization.
All ran once. Runtime, custody, post-run verification, and fresh exact request
replay passed.

## What changed

The acquisition contrast replicated exactly at the method level across both
seeds:

```text
C0
  complete 970-line target reconstruction
  -> enumerate first designated source directory
  -> read one RESULTS.md
  -> capacity

S1
  use structural region reads
  -> acquire all four designated RESULTS/audit records
  -> acquire selected target regions
  -> continue acquiring target context
  -> capacity
```

Aggregated across the two seeds, S1 reduced distinct target-line acquisition
69.6% and raw target bytes 66.4%. It also increased exact governing-source
coverage from one of four files per C0 cell to all four files per S1 cell.
Counting target plus designated-source bytes, C0 acquired 132,078 raw bytes
across its two cells and S1 acquired 97,250, a 26.4% reduction despite S1's
complete source coverage.

This is stronger than “the map was present.” Both S1 actors used
`read_region`; the model-facing address space changed their actual acquisition
paths. It remains a package-level effect. The study does not isolate outline
exposure, stable region IDs, heading text, exact ranges, or the region-read
operation.

## What did not change

The structure package did not produce an executable bounded working state.
Neither S1 actor mutated before capacity. Seed 42 had already received all
four exact governing records plus the phase-continuity and design-consequence
regions while a full response still fit, but it next opened the remaining
unknowns region and then the document header. Seed 314159 received all four
governing records and three broad late-document regions but had not opened the
design-consequences region when capacity stopped it.

The investigator-qualified route proves that legal source, bounded target,
mutation, inspection, and submission operations could fit in 11–12 actions.
It does **not** prove that the route contained a semantically sufficient basis
or that Qwen should have accepted it. The supported statement is narrower:

> The mechanical outline changed Qwen's acquisition policy, but it did not
> make the actor form, accept, and act from a bounded basis before capacity.

S1's initial prompt was 6,178 tokens versus 1,964 for C0: the resident outline
cost 4,214 prompt tokens before acquisition. Total serialized token traffic
was higher under S1, while processed prompt tokens were nearly equal and
recorded model time was lower. The shorter S1 completions and prefix-cache
behavior are plausible contributors, but this comparison does not isolate a
single timing mechanism. Do not call S1 categorically cheaper or faster.

## Apparatus disposition

The comparison instantiated the intended environment:

- each task-designated directory was enumerable through the model-facing
  provider;
- all four governing files were exactly retrievable and literally delivered;
- target pagination and region reads were byte-exact and version-bound;
- the structural tool was the only tool-surface difference;
- the live tokenizer qualified both legal witness paths before inference;
- all accepted actions were rendered into the following request;
- saved prompts regenerated byte-for-byte for all 42 model boundaries; and
- the server reported 25,088 context and full 66/66 offload.

Unlike the predecessor decomposition v0 bank, this result is not invalidated
by source reachability or delivery.

## Evidence decision

Supported on this one authentic overload locus and two matched seeds:

- compact mechanical structural addressability can materially alter Qwen3.8's
  acquisition path;
- the observed change favored complete governing-source coverage and much
  lower target materialization; and
- selective acquisition alone did not yield construction within the resident
  inline-map/native-history execution envelope.

Not supported:

- that the acquired S1 set was semantically sufficient;
- that Qwen ignored a known-sufficient basis;
- that the task intrinsically required the complete target;
- any artifact-quality, verification, repair, or closure comparison;
- a general structural-addressability policy or default resident map;
- a causal claim about which structural component mattered; or
- an automatic successor involving semantic digestion, nonresident maps,
  forced commitment, or cross-context state.

Structural addressability remains a candidate larger-world navigability
layer, now with a replicated upstream effect on this anchor but without local
operability or transfer evidence. The project pauses for user review before
selecting any successor.
